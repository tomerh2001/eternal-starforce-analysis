Method & data

Model, numbers, receipts

  • Model. Star count is a Markov chain: tap → success (+1★), maintain (stay), or destroy (pay the spare cost, restore from trace: 15–19★→12★, 20★→15★, 21★→17★). The optimal per-star policy comes from value iteration on the exact expectations (no simulation involved); each named strategy is solved exactly as a linear system. Distributions come from a live Monte-Carlo (50k runs per strategy).
  • The event. Modeled per v.269: 30% off enhancement cost (verified in-game as multiplicative on the full mode cost; Safeguard surcharge excluded) and — for the full event — 30% lower destruction chance at ≤21★, applied on top of mode rates.
  • Rates. Official GMS v.264 revamp table (fails never drop stars; no boom below 15★). Enhancement Mode Level 2–4 rates and cost multipliers are community-measured in-game (v.269 calculator): ×1.5/×2.5/×3 at 15–17★, ×2/×3.5/×6.5 at 18–21★ with success cut 15→12/10/8% (20★: 30→25/20/15%).
  • Costs. Meso formula verified in-game at Lv 250 (18→19★ base 632.9M; ×6.5 = 4.114b matches). MVP discount applies to the base portion for taps ≤16★; the Safeguard surcharge is never discounted; Star Catch = ×1.05 success on every tap when enabled.

The weekly free Trace Restoration (Eternal armor = 2,312 pts vs ~126 pts/week cap) is excluded — bank it to save one spare every few months. The rarer 5/10/15 Sunny Sunday (100% at 15→16★) is not modeled. Costs assume a non-Superior item.
