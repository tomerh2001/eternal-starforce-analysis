The answer

Protection is only worth buying at 20★ and 21★ — and only Level 3

0–14★: no boom risk exists — just tap. “None” = Enhancement Mode Level 1. Never use classic Safeguard or Level 4 anywhere at a 2b spare price.

The logic: a boom is only as scary as what it costs you. Boom at 15–19★ → trace restores at 12★, and rebuilding 12→15 is pocket change, so you eat the 2b and keep tapping. Boom at 21★ → restore at 17★, and re-climbing 17→21 through the expensive 18–19★ taps runs ~17b — that risk is worth paying down. Level 4 is never worth it: 6.5× cost and success cut to 8% is insurance priced like a scam.

Strategy comparison

The old “safeguard 15–17” habit now costs you 13.7b

Under the revamp, fails keep your stars and a 15–17★ boom only sets you back ~4–5b all-in. Paying +200% per tap to insure against that is the worst habit to carry over. Full protection everywhere costs 2.6× the optimal strategy.

Method & data

Model, numbers, receipts

  • Model. Star count is a Markov chain: tap → success (+1★), maintain (stay), or destroy (pay 2b, restore from trace: 15–19★→12★, 20★→15★, 21★→17★). Expected cost per policy solved exactly (linear system); all 4⁷ = 16,384 per-star policies enumerated; optimum cross-checked with value iteration and a 300k-trial Monte Carlo.
  • Rates. Official GMS v.264 revamp table (fails never drop stars; no boom below 15★). Enhancement Mode Level 2–4 rates and cost multipliers are community-measured in-game (v.269 calculator), including the success-rate cuts at 18–21★ and multipliers ×1.5/×2.5/×3 (15–17★), ×2/×3.5/×6.5 (18–21★).
  • Costs. Meso formula verified in-game at Lv 250 (18→19★ base 632.9M; ×6.5 = 4.114b matches). MVP Black −10% on the base portion for taps ≤16★; Safeguard surcharge is never discounted; guild altar star catch = ×1.05 success, applied to every tap.
