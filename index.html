Method & data

Model, numbers, receipts

  • Model. Tap → success / maintain / destroy (pay the spare, restore at 12★ / 15★ / 17★). Optimal policy via exact value iteration; each strategy solved as a linear system; spreads from a live 50k-run Monte Carlo.
  • Event. Per v.269: 30% off the full mode cost (in-game verified; Safeguard surcharge excluded) and, for the full event, 30% lower destruction ≤21★.
  • Rates. Base rates from the official v.264 notes; Mode 2–4 rates and multipliers (×1.5/2.5/3 at 15–17★, ×2/3.5/6.5 at 18–21★, success cut to 12/10/8%) community-measured in-game.
  • Costs. Meso formula verified at Lv 250 (18→19★ Mode 4 = 4.114b matches in-game). MVP discounts the base portion ≤16★; Star Catch = ×1.05 success.

The rarer 5/10/15 Sunny Sunday (100% at 15→16★) is not modeled. Costs assume a non-Superior item.
